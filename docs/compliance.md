# Competency record checks

Sources checked 8 November 2026. This software finds evidence gaps. It cannot determine competence, approve equipment operation or certify compliance.

## NZ-GRWM-9

[WorkSafe: information, training, instruction and supervision](https://www.worksafe.govt.nz/managing-health-and-safety/businesses/general-requirements-for-workplaces/providing-information-training-instruction-or-supervision-for-workers/) explains the duty to provide suitable and adequate information, training, instruction and supervision under the General Risk and Workplace Management Regulations 2016. Training must fit the work and risks. Worker understanding and competent supervision matter.

The command identifies missing, pending, revoked, expired and below-level assessments for current role requirements. This is an internal record check supporting a supervisor's review. It does not prove a breach of regulation 9. WorkSafe explicitly says training records are not required under this general guidance, though they are useful evidence. Industry-specific rules can impose additional obligations.

## RECORD-EVIDENCE

Approved assessments missing an assessor or a required evidence reference are listed. Our evidence requirement is an internal operating rule, not a statutory retention period. The importer and new approvals reject missing required evidence; legacy data remains visible to the check. The deliberately incomplete demo record proves the check can find it.

## Internal rules

The business configures minimum skill levels and cover targets. A five-level scale, a sixty-day renewal window and a thirty-day expiry projection are demo operating choices, not legal thresholds. Expiry is inclusive through the recorded date. An expired latest assessment does not revive an earlier pass. An assessment review is not a legally validated electronic signature. Training completion never marks a skill competent.

Before using this for real work, a competent supervisor checks the role requirements, equipment-specific authorisations, permitted evidence, individual circumstances and any jurisdiction-specific rules. Do not import medical or sensitive employment information that is not needed for this purpose. Keep the generated documents and backups private.
